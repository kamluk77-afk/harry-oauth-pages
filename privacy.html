Privacy Policy

Harry Backup Production

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Purpose

Harry Backup Production is a personal utility for creating, storing, and restoring encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive account.

2. Google Drive access

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create and manage files used by the backup system.

3. Data processing

Backup archives are encrypted locally using Restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. Backup data is processed for backup and restoration purposes.

4. Data storage and sharing

Encrypted backups are stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner. The application does not intentionally share backup contents with third parties. Google Drive processes stored data under Google's applicable terms and policies.

5. Credentials and security

OAuth credentials are stored in protected local configuration files. Restic encryption protects the contents of uploaded backups.

6. Data retention and deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the owner or by an explicitly authorized backup maintenance operation. The owner may revoke application access through Google Account settings.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application's developer using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
